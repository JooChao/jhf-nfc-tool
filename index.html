Privacy Policy

JHF NFC Tool • Updated October 4, 2026

NFC data

The app reads and writes ISO15693 tags at your request. Tag identifiers (UID), manufacturer information, memory information and block contents are processed on your device. The current app does not include developer-operated data upload, advertising, analytics or account registration.

Local preferences

Language, byte display order, and the server address and port you enter are saved in the app’s local preferences. These settings may be included in backups managed by your operating system. Tag results are held in memory rather than saved to a tag history database.

Optional server forwarding

NFC functions work without a server connection. If you configure a server and tap Connect, subsequent tag information and full/range results, including UID, raw tag contents, request identifiers and timestamps, are automatically sent to that server until you disconnect or the connection ends. This can include partial full-read results and range write/clear verification results. This feature is intended for servers on your local network; the address you enter determines the recipient. The developer does not operate the receiving server.

Transmission and your control

The current forwarding protocol is plain TCP without TLS encryption. Only connect to servers and networks you trust. Disconnect in Result Forwarding to stop transmission; do not connect to keep processing on the device. NFC and local network access use system permissions where applicable. Change permissions in iOS Settings. Server retention and deletion are controlled by the server operator; disconnecting cannot erase data already sent.

Retention and deletion

Reading results are cleared as operations and pages reset or when the app process ends. Delete the app to remove its local app data; operating system backups are managed separately. Contact the receiving server operator for deletion of forwarded data.

Policy and contact

Changes to data handling will be reflected in an updated policy. Developer: JooChao. For privacy questions, contact zzyself@163.com.

隐私政策

JHF NFC Tool • 更新日期：2026 年 10 月 4 日

NFC 数据

应用按您的操作读取和写入 ISO15693 标签，在设备上处理标签 UID、厂商信息、存储信息和块内容。当前应用未集成开发者运营的数据上传、广告、统计分析或账户注册。

本地设置

语言、字节显示顺序及您输入的服务器地址和端口保存在应用本地设置中。这些设置可能包含在操作系统管理的备份中。标签结果保存在内存中，当前没有标签历史数据库。

可选服务器转发

NFC 功能不需要服务器连接。您配置服务器并点击连接后，后续标签信息、整块和范围结果会自动发送到该服务器，包含 UID、原始标签内容、请求标识和时间戳，直至您断开或连接结束。这可能包含整块读取的部分结果以及范围写入、清空的回读校验结果。该功能用于连接局域网服务器，实际接收方由您输入的地址决定，开发者不运营接收服务器。

传输方式与控制

当前转发使用未经过 TLS 加密的 TCP。请仅连接可信的服务器和网络。可在“结果转发”中断开以停止发送；不连接时，标签处理留在本机。NFC 与局域网访问在适用时使用系统权限，可在 iOS 设置中调整。接收服务器的数据保留和删除由其运营者管理，断开连接不会删除已发送的数据。

保留与删除

读取结果随操作和页面重置或应用进程结束而清除。删除应用可移除其本地应用数据，系统备份需另行管理。已转发数据的删除请联系接收服务器运营者。

政策更新与联系

数据处理方式变化时会更新本政策。开发者：JooChao。隐私问题联系邮箱：zzyself@163.com。
